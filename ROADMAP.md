# Product roadmap / 产品路线图

Updated: 2026-09-30. Status refers to the live BNB Smart Chain product. Plans may change after technical and security review.

| Area | Current state | Next work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BSC token launch | Launch and trading interfaces are live | Improve token discovery and migration transparency |
| Perpetual market | BSC market is live with operational controls; current public config reports a 1× maximum | Improve risk controls, oracle handling, and market operations before expanding leverage |
| Genesis subscription | Subscription and chain payment flows are available | Improve claim visibility and public history |
| On-chain history | Indexed transaction and position records are available in the app | Improve loading and presentation across desktop and mobile |
| Referral program | BSC Pump invitation and binding flow exists | Clarify valid referral metrics and reporting; no cash rebates are planned for this phase |
| Quote asset flexibility | USDT and other configured quote assets are supported in selected flows | Research collateral or liquidity pools denominated in arbitrary tokens, subject to oracle and risk review |

## 中文

此路线图记录官网现状与后续方向。永续市场的当前公开配置最高杠杆为 **1×**；更高杠杆须先完成风控与技术评估。使用任意代币作为计价或抵押资产仍属研究事项，需要先解决价格池和 Oracle 风险。路线图不是上线承诺，实际功能以官网和链上配置为准。
